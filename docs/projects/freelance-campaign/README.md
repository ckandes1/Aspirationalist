## Purpose

Build a repeatable path to freelance and fractional work by testing demand, creating useful market-facing content, developing targeted offers, and engaging directly with qualified prospects.

The campaign should generate evidence about which problems buyers will pay to solve, which audiences respond, and which offers are worth developing further.

## Current focus

The initial focus is strategic product marketing and analyst relations work for B2B SaaS companies, including:

- Gartner Magic Quadrant strategy and execution
- Market intelligence and competitive insight
- Positioning and business thesis development
- Executive alignment around market direction
- Upstream product marketing strategy

## Core workstreams

### 1. Offer development

Develop and refine clear freelance and fractional offers based on real buyer problems. Start with focused diagnostic or advisory work that can create value quickly and produce evidence for future paid engagements.

### 2. Organic campaign

Use LinkedIn and other appropriate channels to publish useful ideas, test messaging, create conversations, and identify people or companies with relevant problems.

### 3. Customer and market discovery

Conduct interviews, review market signals, and use early engagements to validate demand, refine the target audience, and improve the offer.

### 4. Targeted outbound and ABM

Identify specific companies or prospects where there is a strong fit and engage them directly with a relevant reason to talk.

#### product.ai projects

Apply to at least one relevant product.ai freelance contract project each week.

This is the first recurring ABM-style motion within the freelance campaign. Each listed project represents a specific prospect with an active need, so the application should be treated as targeted outreach rather than a generic application.

For each relevant opportunity:

1. Evaluate fit with the strategic product marketing and analyst relations thesis.
2. Research the company, project, and likely business problem.
3. Tailor the application to the specific need and demonstrate relevant expertise.
4. Record the opportunity, application date, positioning used, response, and outcome.
5. Identify any useful follow-up beyond the application itself, such as connecting with the company, engaging with relevant people, or developing content around the underlying problem.

This activity also contributes to the separate job-search project because product.ai projects can create either contract work or a path toward a full-time role. It is currently the only recurring activity intentionally serving both projects.

## Weekly operating cadence

- Review new product.ai projects once each week.
- Apply to at least one high-fit project when a credible match is available.
- Complete one meaningful campaign action in each active workstream.
- Record results, signals, and next actions.
- Adjust the campaign based on evidence rather than activity volume alone.

## Measures of progress

Track:

- Qualified conversations created
- Freelance or advisory opportunities identified
- product.ai projects reviewed and applied to
- Responses and interviews generated
- Pilot or paid engagements won
- Repeated buyer problems discovered
- Messaging or offer changes based on evidence
- Full-time opportunities influenced by freelance campaign activity
