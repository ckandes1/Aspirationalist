# Week 1 content launch plan

**Week:** August 3–7, 2026  
**Target:** Publish the first reworked AR campaign post on Thursday, August 6. Friday, August 7 is the fallback.

## Critical outcomes

### 1. Minimum viable response pipeline

Before publishing, establish a simple place for an interested reader to take a next step.

It must support:

- contacting Keith directly;
- expressing interest or joining a waitlist for future help;
- accessing or downloading a relevant resource.

This can be provisional. It does not require a complete website or automated funnel before the first post.

### 2. CTA plan

Choose the CTA structure for the first post and the next several posts.

The CTA must:

- create a clear next step for interested readers;
- avoid overt promotion that could create employer-related risk;
- fit the interview-first campaign strategy;
- connect to the response pipeline;
- allow some posts to prioritize reach and conversation without forcing a direct offer.

Decide whether the first post uses a direct invitation, a resource-oriented CTA, a research/interview invitation, a low-friction contact path, or no explicit CTA beyond profile and comment engagement.

### 3. First post

Rework proven material into a new post with a specific angle rather than reposting the previous article.

Complete:

- angle selection;
- draft;
- CTA insertion or deliberate CTA omission;
- final review for employer separation;
- publication Thursday, with Friday as fallback.

## Sequence

1. Define the minimum response pipeline.
2. Choose the CTA approach.
3. Select the first post angle.
4. Draft and review the post.
5. Publish.
6. Record performance and qualitative responses as a dated source observation.

## Deferred

The following do not need to be resolved before the first post:

- final website versus Substack decision;
- complete five-week editorial calendar;
- polished paid offer;
- automated nurture sequence;
- full resource library;
- complete campaign infrastructure.
