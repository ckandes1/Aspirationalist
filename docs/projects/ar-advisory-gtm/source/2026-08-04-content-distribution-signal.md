# Content distribution signal — 2026-08-04

## Observation

A recently published article is performing unusually well. This reinforces that the underlying AR/MQ subject matter and Keith's perspective can attract meaningful attention.

The result should be treated as a useful signal rather than a complete content strategy. One strong post does not establish a repeatable formula, but it increases confidence that the broader campaign direction is worth executing.

## Working implications

### Rework proven ideas instead of simply reposting them

The same core material can support multiple posts. Each version should create a distinct reason to engage by changing the angle, isolating one specific idea, sharpening the audience, or applying the insight to a different situation.

A five-week sequence of roughly five to ten meaningfully reworked posts creates enough surface area for several to outperform without depending on every post succeeding.

### Build a pipeline rather than treating each post as a one-off

The practical next step is a repeatable content pipeline that turns strong source material into multiple differentiated posts spaced over time.

The pipeline also needs somewhere for interested people to go after seeing a post. It should support at least three paths:

- contact Keith directly;
- express interest or wait for follow-up;
- access or download a useful resource.

The exact mechanism remains open, but it must exist before the first campaign post is published.

### Reconsider the long-form publishing home

The initial thought was to republish or adapt the article on Substack. A personal website may be more shareable among executives and may better support the long-term goal of building an owned body of work. This is an unresolved distribution question, not a decision.

Questions to test later:

- Are target AR leaders and executives more likely to share an article hosted on a personal website than a Substack post?
- Does Substack provide enough discovery, subscription, or publishing convenience to outweigh that concern?
- Should the personal website be the canonical home while Substack is used as a distribution or subscription layer?

## Current conclusion

The campaign concept is directionally strong and should move into execution now.

The first-week priorities are:

1. establish a minimum viable response pipeline;
2. decide the CTA approach and how it avoids employer-related risk;
3. prepare and publish the first reworked post.

Target the first post for Thursday, August 6, 2026, with Friday, August 7 as the fallback.
