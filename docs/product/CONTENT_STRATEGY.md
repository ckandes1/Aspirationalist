# Content Strategy

## Purpose

Define how Aspirationalist turns practical expertise, personal learning, and future-oriented ideas into a coherent body of work.

## Core Content Strategy

Aspirationalist uses practical content as the entry point and a broader optimistic builder worldview as the destination.

People may arrive through a specific problem, such as Gartner, analyst relations, product marketing, AI agents, building as a non-coder, or career switching.

They stay because the broader work helps them learn, build, contribute, and increase their impact.

## Canonical Publishing Strategy

Aspirationalist should prioritize owned canonical resources and artifacts.

Canonical resources are the durable source-of-truth pages on the site. They can be updated, improved, shared, linked, gated, bundled, or monetized over time.

Distributed content across LinkedIn, newsletters, Substack, video, podcasts, or other channels should point back to these canonical resources when relevant.

The current model is:

Canonical resource
→ distributed content
→ inbound attention
→ deeper relationship through newsletter, community, resources, or products

## Content Layers

### 1. Practical Resources

Useful, concrete artifacts that help people solve immediate problems.

Examples:
- Gartner briefing guides
- Analyst relations templates
- PMM frameworks
- Market intelligence templates
- AI agent workflows
- Non-coder building guides
- Career switching resources

### 2. Strategic Essays

Deeper thinking about markets, technology, work, builders, and progress.

Examples:
- Corporate thesis
- Market evolution
- AI adoption
- Builder mindset
- Strategic product marketing
- Future of work and contribution

### 3. Aspirationalist Essays

The broader worldview behind the platform.

Examples:
- Optimism
- Stewardship
- Survival and progress
- Human potential
- Agency
- Meaningful work
- Building a better future

### 4. Personal Field Notes

Personal essays and updates that connect the work to lived experience.

Examples:
- Learning
- Travel
- Fitness
- Sobriety
- Career growth
- Building in public
- Personal experiments

## Content Pillars

- Analyst Relations
- Gartner
- Product Marketing
- Market Intelligence
- AI Agents
- Building as a Non-Coder
- Career Switching
- Aspirationalism
- Personal Field Notes

## Publishing Principle

Publish useful artifacts first.

Use practical resources to earn trust, then connect readers to the larger body of work over time.

## Working Assumption

The content strategy will evolve based on what resonates, what attracts the right people, and what helps the platform build momentum.
