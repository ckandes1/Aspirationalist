# Audience

## Who This Is For

People who are optimistic about the future and want to help build it.

They are creators, builders, tinkerers, artists, operators, founders, and lifelong learners who believe humanity can continue making meaningful progress.

They want to contribute.

They want to create more than they consume.

They want to develop themselves so they can have greater impact on the people and communities around them.

## Common Traits

- Optimistic about the future
- Purpose-driven
- Curious
- Action-oriented
- Interested in growth
- Interested in learning
- Interested in building
- Interested in contributing
- Willing to experiment
- Believe progress is possible

## Common Interests

- Technology
- Business
- AI
- Innovation
- Entrepreneurship
- Human potential
- Productivity
- Learning
- Markets
- Science
- Creativity

## Professional Examples

Many members of this audience work in technology, including:

- Founders
- Product Marketers
- Market Intelligence professionals
- Analyst Relations professionals
- Industry Analysts
- Evangelists
- Product Leaders
- Marketing Leaders
- Solopreneurs
- Content Creators

However, technology is not a requirement.

The defining characteristic is a desire to build, contribute, and help create a better future.

## Desired Outcomes

- Greater impact
- Better decisions
- Faster learning
- Stronger relationships
- Increased capability
- Meaningful contribution
- A life connected to purpose
