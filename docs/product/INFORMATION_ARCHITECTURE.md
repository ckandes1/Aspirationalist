# Information Architecture

## Purpose

Define the initial site structure for Aspirationalist.

This document explains how canonical resources, topic hubs, projects, and future apps should be organized so the site can grow without becoming a junk drawer.

## Core Model

Aspirationalist is built around owned canonical resources and topic hubs.

External channels such as LinkedIn, newsletter, Substack, video, or podcasts are used for distribution. They should point back to durable resources and topic pages on the site.

## Primary Navigation

- Home
- Resources
- Topics
- Projects
- About

## Home

Purpose:
Introduce Aspirationalist, communicate the strategic foundation, and route visitors to the most useful next step.

Primary actions:
- Explore resources
- Browse topics
- Subscribe to the newsletter
- Learn what Aspirationalist is

## Resources

Purpose:
Offer practical, durable artifacts people can use.

Examples:
- Templates
- Guides
- Frameworks
- Checklists
- Playbooks
- Downloadable assets
- Canonical explainers

Most resources should be free. Some may require a free account. Some may become paid.

## Topics

Purpose:
Organize content and resources by subject area.

Initial topics:
- Analyst Relations
- Gartner
- Product Marketing
- Market Intelligence
- AI Agents
- Building as a Non-Coder
- Career Switching
- Aspirationalism
- Personal Field Notes

Each topic page should include:
- Short overview
- Best starting point
- Featured resources
- Related posts or distributed content
- Related projects
- Newsletter or community CTA

## Projects

Purpose:
Show active experiments, software tools, web apps, research projects, and build-in-public updates.

This is where Aspirationalist becomes more than content.

## About

Purpose:
Explain Keith, Aspirationalist, the strategic foundation, and the broader vision.

## Open Decision: Articles

Aspirationalist may not need Articles as a primary navigation item in v1.

Long-form writing may initially be published through external channels and then summarized, linked, or repurposed on topic and resource pages.

Future decision:
Decide whether Aspirationalist becomes the canonical home for long-form articles or remains the owned hub that organizes writing published elsewhere.

## Working Assumption

The initial navigation should stay simple.

The site should use topics, tags, and resource types instead of too many top-level categories.
