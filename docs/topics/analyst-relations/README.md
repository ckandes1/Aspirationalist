# Analyst Relations

This topic covers the strategy, practice, and intelligence function of Analyst Relations — with a focus on how AR drives business outcomes, not just MQ placement.

## What we're building toward

A body of work that reframes AR as an intelligence function that feeds business strategy, not a communications function that megaphones it.

## Content maturity stages

Raw → Frameworks → Assets

- **raw/** — unpolished notes, session captures, stream of consciousness. Source material only.
- **frameworks/** — formalized models and named concepts developed from raw material.
- **assets/** — canonical, publishable content ready for the site.

Do not publish from `raw/`. Do not skip `frameworks/` when building `assets/`.
