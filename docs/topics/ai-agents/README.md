# AI Agents

This topic covers how to work with AI agents effectively — tools, workflows, operating systems, and real-world experimentation.

## What we're building toward

Practical, honest content from someone who experiments with AI on real projects and shares what actually works.

## Content maturity stages

Raw → Frameworks → Assets

- **raw/** — unpolished notes, drafts, session captures
- **frameworks/** — formalized models and named concepts
- **assets/** — canonical, publishable content ready for the site

Do not publish from `raw/`. Do not skip `frameworks/` when building `assets/`.
