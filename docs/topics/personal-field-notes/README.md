# Personal Field Notes

This topic captures lived experiences, observations, and early lessons about work, growth, decision-making, execution, and building toward a better future.

## What we're building toward

Practical frameworks and artifacts grounded in real experience rather than abstract advice.

Notes may begin as personal situations, frustrations, or discoveries. Over time, some may mature into named frameworks, feed a larger project, or become useful artifacts for other people.

## Content maturity

`raw → frameworks → assets`

- **raw/** contains dated notes, experiences, early models, and unresolved thinking.
- **frameworks/** contains deliberately named and formalized concepts developed from multiple sources or tested experience.
- **assets/** contains outputs intentionally designed for other people to use.

A raw note should preserve the experience and emerging mechanism without presenting the idea as settled guidance.
