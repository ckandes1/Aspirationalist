## One-minute briefing for the next AI

Keith wants to build a deeply developed, research-informed fictional future of civilization shaped by AI, automation and progressively reliable access to basic human needs. The work should expose the causal logic of an optimistic, aspirational worldview through independent, compelling stories or other media. The analogy is a Star Trek-scale universe coupled with a Dave Ramsey-style distribution strategy: each individual piece delivers standalone value, while encounters with more pieces reveal an interconnected model.

Keith is asking for an AI-executed, multi-stage **research, worldbuilding, publishing-strategy and project-design program**. He is the directing decision-maker. Different AIs may research, draft, criticize and propose options over months or years. The long-term repository is the shared context, decisions, and work product. Project stages should change as discoveries emerge, with explicit review gates before conclusions are promoted into the approved framework or world canon.

**Immediate objective:** Preserve this brief, audit existing sources, then commission a bounded Stage 0 project-architecture study. Do not start writing a manifesto, a novel, or custom agent-orchestration software. Do not create a formal project or fill in Keith's hypothesis and kill criteria without his decision.

## The originating problem and causal chain

1. The idea is too interconnected and expansive to communicate all at once. People can understand specific elements before they are ready to appreciate the whole.
2. An independently engaging piece about one aspect of the future can become an entry point into the larger model. Many pieces acquire additional meaning because their assumptions, causes, and consequences fit together.
3. To make those connections authentic, the civilization needs common foundations: economic mechanisms, institutions, technology, psychology, cultural norms, power and geopolitics, and the timelines on which they change.
4. A single broad story is insufficient. Different people, at different locations and historical moments, will experience the same transitions differently. Rural water access and U.S.-China competition provide contrasting entry points.
5. The author does not yet know the right research methodology, level of worldbuilding, publishing medium, or program sequence. Those alternatives must be analyzed before locking in a playbook.
6. The investigation may exceed one model's context and run across many sessions, tools and providers. If findings cannot be stored and reviewed, context loss and premature decisions will compound.
7. The Aspirationalist repository *already* solves the generic cross-session memory and GitHub change-review problem. The conversation repeatedly corrected proposals to rebuild infrastructure. The next useful work is the domain-specific research/program architecture and its initial execution using existing conventions.

## Intended outcome, stated provisionally

- The Aspirationalist philosophical thesis and its foundational, testable propositions, with evidence, objections, assumptions and unresolved claims.
- A plausible, coherent civilization spanning defined historical bookends and intermediate transitions, including competing scenarios when needed.

- A creative architecture through which individual stories, essays, audio/video, novels or other formats work independently and cumulatively communicate a broader worldview.
- A comparative, stack-ranked strategy for building and distributing that body of work, backed by research and small pilots.
- A repeatable AI-supported workflow for producing, auditing, approving, revising and publishing material, without tying the project to a single model provider.

## Foundational distinctions to preserve

### Aspirationalism and fiction serve different jobs

The philosophy is a set of propositions about what humans can do, which values matter and how progress may occur. The fictional universe is a plausible scenario space where those propositions, their consequences and their failures can be explored. A coherent fictional world does not prove a philosophical claim; evidence and objections must be evaluated separately. The fiction should be allowed to challenge the worldview.

### Historical bookends are approximate gates

Keith proposed an **early bookend** near the point when basic needs have become broadly accessible. Necessities being met remains distinguishable from reliable resolution of threats to survival. A **later bookend** lies in a society "chasing the nines": people regard survival security as normal, and predictable tragedy from avoidable circumstance becomes increasingly rare. The transition may shift human effort from survival toward stewardship, exploration, relationships and chosen aspirations. Neither the calendar years nor the exact geographic scope of either bookend has been set.

The timeline must support multiple viewpoints. The same year may be experienced very differently in different regions. Physical automation, clean water infrastructure and healthcare can arrive at different rates, mediated by governance, distribution, conflict, resources and trust.

### Survival, pain, tragedy and choice

Keith is interested in the boundary between circumstantial suffering and suffering connected to chosen ambitions or tradeoffs. This is an exploratory philosophical distinction, **not** a claim that all sadness, coercion, illness, accidents or conflict can disappear. Dramatic tension in a mature-abundance setting remains an open creative and plausibility question.

### Money and scarcity

Keith floated money disappearing in a fully post-scarcity world. Prior discussion distinguished universal essential abundance from the possible continuing scarcity of land, power, attention, influence, rare materials and experiences. Do not establish a moneyless society as canon without explaining how rivalrous or finite goods are allocated.

### Historical perspectives and conflict

Illustrative lenses raised in conversation:
- An impoverished Zimbabwean community whose water supply still requires a trip to a local well.
- U.S.-China technological, strategic and institutional competition during rapid AI development.
- The political systems that might replace or transform current governance when survival becomes increasingly secure.
- Individual and family psychology, identity, work, status and purpose in societies where economic employment is no longer a survival condition.

These are examples to investigate, not committed plotlines or predetermined outcomes.

## The "Dave Ramsey method" the user means

The intended mechanism is **modular cumulative understanding**. A practical, self-contained item earns attention because it solves or illuminates an immediate problem. Another item makes a different idea useful. Repeated exposure reveals a consistent network of propositions, so each piece increases the value and comprehensibility of the rest. Jordan Peterson was cited as a comparison for interconnected beliefs; this does not imply an endorsement of his substantive positions.

Desired properties:
- Each item can be understood and enjoyed without prerequisite reading.
- The creator can release items across media and in different orders.
- Shared world rules and propositions reward repeat engagement.
- The body of work supports independent entry points and a broader synthesis.
- The fictional stories must have actual characters, conflict and resolution, not merely serve as ideological explanations.
- Internal logical consistency makes the system intelligible; it is not external proof that the world will unfold this way.

The format remains undecided. Candidate outputs include short fiction, sci-fi novels, scripted video, essays, podcasts, articles and hybrid approaches. A future evaluation should rank them against the project's objectives, practical cost, audience appeal, reusable IP and speed of testing.

The exact aspiration-list source was not identified in the conversation and must not be invented.

**Layer B. Civilization/world model.** Define bookends, plausible technological-economic conditions, institutions, ownership and access mechanisms, geopolitics, uneven global diffusion, and period-specific everyday life. Maintain alternative futures where uncertainty is material.

**Layer C. Narrative/publishing strategy.** Investigate different media, story architectures, entry points, continuity rules, production methods, testing and distribution. Evaluate how independently compelling pieces can communicate the interconnected model.

**Layer D. Operating playbook.** Integrate research method, canon policies, story specifications, production workflow, review gates, measurement and iteration.

These layers inform each other. Their order and degree of parallelism are subjects for the Stage 0 architecture study. One candidate is ideology-first; one is world-first; one is iterative co-development. Do not select among these simply because a previous assistant suggested one.

## Program management practices already selected as suitable

Use proven practices rather than inventing orchestration machinery:
- **Rolling-wave planning:** Near-term assignments are precise. Later stages remain provisional and are revised as evidence arrives.
- **Stage gates:** The director approves key hypotheses, canon, format and scope changes before work that depends on them is treated as settled.
- **Kanban-style visibility:** Make work, status, blockers, review and dependencies visible.
- **GitHub branches and pull requests:** A contributor proposes changes for review. Working drafts do not become canonical because an AI produced them.
- **Decision history and impact review:** Upstream changes identify affected downstream material. An approved claim, approved story-world rule and completed publication are different things.

A task may be Backlog, In Progress, Submitted, Under Review, Changes Requested, Approved, Integrated, or Revalidation Required. This is a **candidate vocabulary**; avoid adding it as a duplicate workflow unless a genuine gap is identified.

Parallel research is initiated by Keith or an authorized orchestrator. There is no requirement for autonomous self-dispatch, background activity or a custom agent platform today.
