# Aspirationalism as a methodology: working definition

Status: Raw topic note, not canonical guidance  
Captured: 2026-07-08  
Potential direction: The evolving definition and operating methodology at the center of the Aspirationalist Project

## Working definition

Aspirationalism is the optimistic belief that humanity has the capacity to influence the future and build a better one, paired with the desire to contribute productively toward that future as individuals and together.

This is a working definition. It is intended to improve over time.

## What seems clear now

Aspirationalism begins with optimism about human capacity.

Humanity is capable of influencing what the future becomes. A better future is possible, and people can help build it.

That belief creates a reason to contribute. Individuals can act productively. People can coordinate their effort. Collective action can create more progress than isolated effort.

The emotional posture is hopeful, ambitious, and urgent: humanity can come together and do this, so let's go. LFG.

## Why this is a methodology topic

Aspirationalism is intended to become more than a broad belief about progress.

The Aspirationalist Project should develop the principles, practices, standards, and tools that translate the belief into useful action. The current working definition captures the mindset and intention. The operational method still needs to be discovered and developed.

## Relationship to the Aspirationalist Project

Aspirationalism is the evolving methodology.

The Aspirationalist Project is Keith Andes's body of work for defining, testing, documenting, improving, and sharing it.

The project should preserve uncertainty rather than locking in a polished definition too early. Defining Aspirationalism is part of the work itself.

## Current components

1. **Optimism:** A better future is possible.
2. **Human agency:** Humanity can influence the future.
3. **Individual contribution:** People can choose to contribute productively.
4. **Collective contribution:** People can work together to create greater progress.
5. **Action and momentum:** The belief should produce movement toward building the future.

These components are provisional. They help identify the current shape without claiming that the methodology is complete.

## Open questions

- Is optimism the foundation, or is responsibility the foundation?
- Does Aspirationalism claim that humanity can solve any problem, or that humanity should continue trying to expand what is solvable?
- What does productive contribution require in practice?
- What principles govern responsible action toward a better future?
- How should the methodology handle disagreement about what a better future means?
- What role do technology, business, systems of work, capital, talent, and attention play?
- Which practices make Aspirationalism usable by an individual?
- Which practices make it usable by a team, company, community, or society?
- What are the failure modes of optimism without evidence, discipline, or responsibility?
- When is the definition mature enough to move from raw material into a framework?

## Raw language worth preserving

> An optimistic mindset about humanity having the capacity to influence the future.

> Ultimately, the point is that we can build a better future.

> Having the optimism for that and the desire to productively contribute as individuals and as a team.

> Humanity can come together and do it, and so let's fucking go. LFG.

> I'd call it a working definition, intended to be improved over time.
