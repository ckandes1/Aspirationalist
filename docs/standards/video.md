# Video Standard

## Purpose

Define reusable creative conventions for Aspirationalist video production.

This is a living standard. Add patterns when they become useful enough to reuse across multiple videos. Keep exploratory one-off ideas out until they show repeat value.

## Soundtrack Palette

Build a small palette of tracks and track sections organized by what they do in a video.

Primary use tags:
- Intro
- Low-key background
- Building background
- Transition / drop
- Ending
- Sparse / dialogue-heavy

A track may serve several roles.

### How to add a track

For each soundtrack candidate, capture:
- **Track / artist**
- **Overall vibe:** a short description of the emotional and production feel
- **Public context:** useful genre, artist, or release context from credible public sources when available
- **Best uses:** which palette roles it fits
- **Timestamp notes:** specific sections and what they could do
- **Caveats:** where the energy, density, tone, or structure may make it a poor fit
- **Rights:** verify licensing / usage rights before publishing. Inclusion here means creative fit only.

The vibe description should combine Keith's reaction to the track with useful external context. Do not force a genre label when the better description is experiential.

## Palette

### Mindful — Ronald Jenkees

**Overall vibe:** Energetic, instrumental, keyboard-driven electronic music with a strong sense of forward motion. It feels optimistic, kinetic, and continuously building, with enough variation to support several different video moments.

**Public context:** Ronald Jenkees is generally associated with electronic, instrumental, keyboard, and hip-hop-oriented music, with broader hip-hop and jazz influence in his performance style. "Mindful" was originally released as a single in 2017 and later appeared on *Wonder And Flow*.

**Best uses:** Intro, low-key background, building background, transition / drop, ending, sparse / dialogue-heavy.

**Timestamp notes:**
- **0:01–0:15:** Good full intro.
- **0:10–0:15:** Tighter intro option.
- **0:15–0:30:** Strong general background section.
- **0:30–1:00:** Still works underneath content, with more build and energy.
- **1:00–1:30:** Chiller section, closer to the feel of 0:15–0:30.
- **~1:30:** Strong drop / payoff. Excellent candidate for an ending or decisive transition.
- **~2:30:** Distinctive section worth testing for a transition or change in visual rhythm.
- **~3:00:** Cooldown with a useful build into it.
- **~3:15:** Feels like the "main plot." Strong sustained background build.
- **4:00+:** Much sparser. Potentially useful when dialogue or the spoken point needs more room.

**Caveats:** The track carries a lot of energy and forward motion. That can make it feel powerful and signature-like, but may overpower reflective, intimate, or deliberately quiet material.
